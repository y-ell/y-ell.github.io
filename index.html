Bio

About

I'm a Year 3 Electrical & Electronic Engineering student at Nanyang Technological University, Singapore. My coursework spans signal processing, power electronics, and digital systems, with a growing specialisation in semiconductor process technology and the automation that sits around it.

My career focus is intelligent manufacturing and semiconductor automation — using software and data to make fabrication processes more reliable, efficient, and transparent. I'm drawn to the layer where hardware meets software: real-time embedded systems, industrial communication protocols, and the edge-to-cloud pipelines that turn raw factory signals into actionable insight.

On the technical side I work primarily in Python, with hands-on experience in Modbus RTU and MQTT for industrial device communication, and machine learning applied to sensor data and fault detection. My final-year project investigates multi-modal data analysis for partial discharge diagnosis — combining signal processing and deep learning to improve electrical insulation monitoring in high-voltage equipment.

I spent a semester on exchange at Fudan University in Shanghai, which gave me a ground-level view of semiconductor manufacturing across the region and sharpened my interest in the supply-chain and policy dimensions of the chip industry. Outside the lab I follow fab economics, equipment capex cycles, and the geopolitics of advanced packaging.
